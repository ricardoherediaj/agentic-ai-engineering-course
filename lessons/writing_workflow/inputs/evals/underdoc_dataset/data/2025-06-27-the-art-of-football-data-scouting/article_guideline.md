# Article Guideline

- **Working title:** How data-driven squad building works, with a club case study.
- **Bucket:** explainers
- **Audience:** football analytics practitioners and aspiring analysts
- **Angle:** Explain the scouting mindset and walk through a structured team analysis on a real club, ending in what to look for next.

## Section intents

1. Motivate data in recruitment with a well-known story and clubs that apply it.
2. List the questions that frame a club analysis and the data platform used.
3. Do the overview and metric analysis, then compare against the league.
4. Evaluate the squad phase by phase to find gaps.
5. Close by summarising the gaps and teasing the follow-up.
