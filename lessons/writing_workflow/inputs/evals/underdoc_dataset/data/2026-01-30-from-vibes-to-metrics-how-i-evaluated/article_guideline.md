# Article Guideline

- **Working title:** Moving from eyeballing to measuring a football RAG system.
- **Bucket:** builds
- **Audience:** data and AI practitioners building LLM applications
- **Angle:** Evaluation build log: the adopted methodology, golden answers, the judge setup, scoring rules, an instructive failure and prompt iteration.

## Section intents

1. Admit the earlier informal way of judging output and why it falls short.
2. Introduce the evaluation-first methodology and its source.
3. Explain the reference data used as an answer key.
4. Describe the judge model setup and the scoring dimensions and weights.
5. Show how domain rules are turned into checkable criteria and walk through one failing example.
6. Show the prompt optimisation loop and wrap up with practices learned.
