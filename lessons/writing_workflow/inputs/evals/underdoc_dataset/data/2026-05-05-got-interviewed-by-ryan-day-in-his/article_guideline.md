# Article Guideline

- **Working title:** Extra thoughts after a newsletter interview about side projects and learning.
- **Bucket:** bridge
- **Audience:** people moving into tech or data from another background
- **Angle:** Follow-up to being interviewed: pick a few questions and add the reasoning that did not fit in the interview.

## Section intents

1. Introduce the interviewer and newsletter, and say why three questions deserve more space.
2. Explain why a self-directed project teaches differently from structured study.
3. Describe how he mixes three learning modes and why their order matters, including the traps at each extreme.
4. Say what he is currently running in parallel.
5. Make the case for writing about what you learn, even with a tiny audience, and link the full interview.
