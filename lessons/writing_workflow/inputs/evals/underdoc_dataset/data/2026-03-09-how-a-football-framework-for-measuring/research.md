# Research dossier: question-first performance framework

Facts reconstructed from the published post.

## The framework (Twelve Football Context Engineering course)
- Starting rule: start with football, not data; first frame a human question (what are you trying to answer and for whom?). Source: https://twelve.football/courses/context-engineering-for-football
- Example questions: what makes a good midfielder; what makes a team dangerous in defensive transitions. Source: https://twelve.football/courses/context-engineering-for-football
- Qualities are composite scores built from 3 to 10 metrics, weighted by importance and computed with z-scores against the right comparison group. Source: https://twelve.football/courses/context-engineering-for-football
- Worked example: a Passing Quality score for midfielders from five xT-based metrics (passes, passes into the final third, passes in the final third, crosses, creative passes) weighted 30/20/20/20/10. Source: https://twelve.football/courses/context-engineering-for-football
- Illustration: Kevin De Bruyne, Premier League 2022/2023, scoring near the top and the framework showing which dimension he excels at. Source: https://twelve.football/courses/context-engineering-for-football
- Comparison group should be defined by position, age, league and minutes played; context determines how a metric is read. Source: https://twelve.football/courses/context-engineering-for-football

## Transfer to healthcare (author's day job)
- Diabetic patient monitoring: human question is how well a patient manages blood sugar; clinical measures named are HbA1c, time above range, time below range, trend direction and glucose variability. Source: the published post (author's own clinical context)
- Comparison against patients of similar age, diabetes type and diagnosis duration; the output should be a sentence a physician can act on. Source: https://twelve.football/courses/context-engineering-for-football
- Same template suggested for SaaS churn and credit risk. Source: https://twelve.football/courses/context-engineering-for-football
