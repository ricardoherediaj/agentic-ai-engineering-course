# Article Guideline

- **Working title:** A football measurement framework as a template for any analytics problem.
- **Bucket:** explainers
- **Audience:** data practitioners in any industry
- **Angle:** Explain a question-first way of structuring analysis, why comparison groups matter, and transplant it to a healthcare case from his day job.

## Section intents

1. Confess the old habit of starting from available data and what it produced.
2. Introduce the framework's starting rule and how a human question is turned into a composite score.
3. Explain the role of the comparison group and why it changes how a metric is read.
4. Apply the same template to a clinical monitoring scenario and show what the final output should look like.
5. Mention other fields where it could be reused and close with a short takeaway.
