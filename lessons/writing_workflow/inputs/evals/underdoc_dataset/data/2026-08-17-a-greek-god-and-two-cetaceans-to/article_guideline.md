# Article Guideline

- **Working title:** Trying an open-model coding stack as an alternative to a closed coding agent.
- **Bucket:** builds
- **Audience:** developers experimenting with agentic coding tools and cheaper models
- **Angle:** A practical field report: what the pieces are, how they were installed, how the work was directed and reviewed, what it cost.

## Section intents

1. Light, playful opening that explains why the agent has its name, then state the task given to it.
2. Introduce the three components of the stack and the role of each.
3. Walk through installation and account setup at a practical level.
4. Explain the difference between the harness and the goal loop he had to design, and how the work was reviewed.
5. Report the session cost with a breakdown by model.
6. Draw takeaways about cheap models, persistent session notes and human review, and ask readers about their own experience.
