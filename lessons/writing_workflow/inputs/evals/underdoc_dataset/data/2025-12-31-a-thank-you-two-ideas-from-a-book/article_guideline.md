# Article Guideline

- **Working title:** Year-end note: the book idea that reframed how he builds a career.
- **Bucket:** bridge
- **Audience:** people moving into tech or data from another background
- **Angle:** Personal reflection tying a popular career book to his pivot from medicine; a thank-you to readers framed by one mindset shift.

## Section intents

1. Thank readers and recap in one breath what the newsletter covered this year.
2. Introduce the book, who recommended it, and the belief it made him question.
3. Contrast the two mindsets the book is built around and relate them to his own pivot.
4. Bring in a public-figure example that illustrates deliberate long-term craft.
5. Explain the idea of accumulating rare, valuable skills and how it changed what he chooses to learn.
6. Close with a short plan for the next year and one sentence readers can take away.

## Sources

- Book: So Good They Can't Ignore You (Cal Newport)
