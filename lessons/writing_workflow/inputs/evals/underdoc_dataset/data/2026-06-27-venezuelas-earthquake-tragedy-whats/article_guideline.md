# Article Guideline

- **Working title:** What happened in the Venezuela earthquakes and how to help from abroad.
- **Bucket:** field-notes
- **Audience:** general readers and the Venezuelan diaspora
- **Angle:** A news-and-action post from a diaspora perspective: inform briefly, honour the solidarity, explain context honestly, list vetted ways to help.

## Section intents

1. State what happened, when, and the scale known at the time of writing.
2. Share the personal angle of living abroad and what he saw people do on the ground.
3. Acknowledge the international response.
4. Give his opinion on why the damage was worse than it should have been.
5. Provide practical ways to help from anywhere, with trustworthy links, then close with a short plea to share.
