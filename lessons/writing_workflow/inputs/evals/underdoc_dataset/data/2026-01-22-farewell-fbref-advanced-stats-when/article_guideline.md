# Article Guideline

- **Working title:** Reaction to a major free football data source dropping its advanced metrics.
- **Bucket:** field-notes
- **Audience:** football analytics practitioners and aspiring analysts
- **Angle:** A quick opinion piece on a news event: what was lost, why it hurts, and how the community can adapt.

## Section intents

1. Announce the news and its immediate effect on the community.
2. Explain why the source mattered to learners and analysts, and link his earlier tutorial on it.
3. Point to alternative open data providers and the mindset to adopt when using them.
4. Suggest learning from how professionals publicly present their work.
5. Mention synthetic data as a practice option, then close with a call to build now.
