# Article Guideline

- **Working title:** A weekly learning-diary habit for people working in data.
- **Bucket:** bridge
- **Audience:** people moving into tech or data from another background
- **Angle:** Personal methodology post: why he writes things down, how his notes evolved and the routine that works.

## Section intents

1. Reframe what writing is for beyond big publications.
2. Tell the story of the paper notebook and what it helped with at work.
3. Explain the move to a digital tool and how notes are categorised.
4. Describe the diary routine step by step and the weekly review.
5. Give takeaways and ask readers for their own methods.
