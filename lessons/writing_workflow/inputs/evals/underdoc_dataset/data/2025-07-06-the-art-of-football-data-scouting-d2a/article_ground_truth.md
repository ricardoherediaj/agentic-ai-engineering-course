# The Art of Football Data Scouting (II): 3 Steps to Find Potential Signings

*Spotting Players with Driblab Pro*

In my last article, I talked about how data can help build football squads and how I was using this approach to spot potential signings for Como 1907. I’ve made a shortlist of these players and I’ll go over their profiles and why I think they meet Como’s criteria for season 25/26.

### Quick Recap

To recap, there are **3 steps** I’ve taken to carry out this analysis:

1. **Full Team Analysis**: I’ve analyzed Como’s performance in Serie A 24/25 and how they performed in offensive and defensive phases. I also compared them with the other teams in the league. With this, I wanted to spot areas of improvements within the squad.

2. **Profile Identification**: based on what the team needs, I identified specific profiles that can fulfill that necessity.

3. **Search & Comparison**: For that, I used **Driblab’s** data hub to shortlist interesting players based on specific criteria.

I’ll develop the last 2 points and final conclusions on the players I shortlisted.

### Profile Identification

- **Attacking Left Back:** an attacking fullback with good physical ability, dribbling, and crossing skills to provide more width and depth on the left flank. Alberto Moreno’s contract ends in June 2025, so it’s key to anticipate his possible departure and secure a quality replacement.

- **Attacking Right Back:** an attacking fullback who can strengthen the right side and create synergy with the right winger. Ideally he can carry the ball forward and deliver crosses into the box, but also has good passing quality.

- **Right Winger:** there’s a need for more dribbling and quality crosses from the right wing to diversify the attack and create more goal-scoring chances. Ability to get in behind and provide depth. Would add competition and alternatives to Strefezza.

- **Center Forward:** there’s a need for more physical presence and effectiveness in the box to make the most of crosses and increase chances conversion. A striker who wins aerial duels and is clinical, giving the team more finishing options.

Selected profiles in a 1-4-2-3-1 line up.

### Search & Comparison

After I had identified the profiles, it was time to search for the players.

Driblab has a ‘ **Scout**’ tab that allows to search players worldwide, filtering by different parameters. I only had access to the top 5 leagues, but still it was an awesome setting to test this feature.

Driblab Pro

With this, you can select players and make shortlists, build radar plots of each selected player and compare them with the ones from your squad and pretty much build your own database of selected players.

To search players, I considered: team performance, specific KPIs, and the economic context, something important for clubs, because there’s a budget sporting directions need to stick to. Aspects I looked into:

- Age of recent signings → young players with an average of 26 y/o. Veteran players (over 30) with leadership, signed as free agents or at low cost, mixing experience with youth in the squad.

- Players on loan with an option to buy, or direct transfers/purchases.

- Market value → range from € 100K - € 10M

- Year End Contract → Summer 2025 (preferably)

- Top 5 Leagues: → Serie A, Bundesliga, Premier League, LaLiga, Ligue 1.

- Specific Positions → i.e. Attacking Midfielder Left - AML

- KPIs→ Driblab scores (>45), crosses, passing, minimum minutes played (450’), xG, xA, etc.

#### Shortlisted Players

##### Players Radars and Comparison With Como Players

**Javi Galán Summary**

Left back with great physical ability and the capacity to advance with the ball down the wing. He provides depth on the left, with a high percentage of successful passes and dribbles. Reliable in offensive progression, though he can be impulsive defensively, bringing speed, dribbling, and solidity to the left side.

**Alvaro Tejero Summary**

Attacking and versatile right back. He crosses well, creates chances, and is reliable in building out from the back. While he doesn’t stand out defensively, he would add attacking depth and quality crosses to diversify play down the flanks.

**Junya Ito Summary**

Fast and productive right winger, excellent at beating defenders and delivering crosses. He provides assists and scores from deep positions, also contributing defensively. Although his goal contribution is low, he offers speed and unpredictability on the wing.

**Mbala N’Zola Summary**

Center Forward with a strong presence in the box, excels in physical duels and aerial play. He stands out for his finishing, ability to shield the ball, and pressing high up the pitch. While he doesn’t contribute much in creation or dribbling, he adds finishing, power, and is a key attacking reference in the opponent’s area.

### Final Conclusions

- **Diagnosis and Strategic Solution:** Como 1907’s play is overly centralized, with a heavy reliance on Nico Paz and low efficiency in the opponent’s box. The proposed signings directly address these needs by strengthening the flanks and improving the team’s attacking aerial game.

- **Expected Tactical Impact of the Signings:**

  - **Javi Galán and Álvaro Tejero:** would provide depth and width on both flanks, diversifying attacking options and increasing the quality of crosses into the box.

  - **Junya Ito:** would complement Strefezza, adding unpredictability, speed, and the ability to dominate the right wing.

  - **Mbala N'Zola:** will bring physical presence in the box, improving offensive aerial play and finishing.
- **Economic Viability:** The selected players represent a sustainable investment (range €1.8M–€6M) with potential for increased value, aligned with the club’s recent transfer policy and with contracts that allow for medium-term planning.
