# Article Guideline

- **Working title:** Automating a monthly finance routine with an agent skill.
- **Bucket:** builds
- **Audience:** people who want to automate boring personal workflows with agents
- **Angle:** A personal build story: the manual routine, the tools involved, how the skill is structured and the problems that surfaced.

## Section intents

1. Describe the old manual routine and why it kept falling behind.
2. Introduce the spreadsheet-style app and template he adopted.
3. Explain the idea of the skill and how the two tool servers are used.
4. Give setup guidance for readers.
5. Describe the main problems met and how each was handled.
6. Report a first real run, then offer advice and honest limitations.
