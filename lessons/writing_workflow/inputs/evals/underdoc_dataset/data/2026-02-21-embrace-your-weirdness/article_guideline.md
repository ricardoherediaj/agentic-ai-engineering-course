# Article Guideline

- **Working title:** Why an odd mix of skills is a career advantage in the AI era.
- **Bucket:** bridge
- **Audience:** people moving into tech or data from another background
- **Angle:** Personal essay that pushes back on the 'pick one lane' advice using his own path from medicine to AI, ending with questions readers can ask themselves.

## Section intents

1. Open with the pressure everyone feels to specialise and why he disagrees.
2. Reference a book idea about education, specialists and outliers, with a fair caveat.
3. Tell his own path step by step, including the doubts others voiced and how he took stock of his gaps.
4. Show the intersection of his strengths and why the combination is already a niche.
5. Give a short set of self-reflection prompts readers can write down.
6. Close on what AI will and will not commoditise, and connect back to what the newsletter is about.
