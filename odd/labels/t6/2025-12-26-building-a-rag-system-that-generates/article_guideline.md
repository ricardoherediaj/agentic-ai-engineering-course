# Article Guideline

- **Working title:** Launch post: a RAG app that turns free football event data into tactical reports.
- **Bucket:** builds
- **Audience:** football analytics practitioners and aspiring analysts and developers curious about applied RAG
- **Angle:** Series opener giving the big picture: the problem, what was built, how it works at a high level and what the next posts will cover.

## Section intents

1. Open with a short seasonal check-in and why he is back writing.
2. State the practical pain of collecting free match data from several sites and the ideal interaction he wanted.
3. Present the live demo and what it supports, with headline scale and cost figures.
4. Give the end-to-end flow in simple steps and summarise the main design decisions without deep detail.
5. Report the first evaluation results and be honest about remaining failure modes.
6. Lay out the roadmap of upcoming posts and invite readers to try and clone the project.
